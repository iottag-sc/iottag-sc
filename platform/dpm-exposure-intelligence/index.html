From Monitoring To TARP Action

Turn changing DPM conditions into a structured operational response.

Atlas can support the site's existing Trigger Action Response Plan (TARP) by bringing the information required to understand a trigger and coordinate the defined response into one operational view.

TARP State What Atlas Identifies Site-Defined Response
Normal DPM and controls within expected operating conditions Continue operations and monitor
Elevated Increasing DPM trend or emerging hotspot Investigate source, personnel exposure and control performance
Action Site-defined trigger reached or control degraded Apply defined engineering / operational controls
Escalation Conditions continue to deteriorate Escalate, restrict activity or remove personnel as defined by the TARP
Recovery Conditions return within site-defined recovery criteria Verify controls and return according to site procedure
